# Real views through 8-inch Celestron telescopes (ADR-107)

Photographs and one film by amateur astronomers, from Wikimedia Commons. They show what
a Celestron C8 / NexStar 8SE shows; none was taken by this observatory. Each is cropped
and re-encoded here; the film is ten seconds of the original, desaturated. Works under
CC BY-SA stay under CC BY-SA in their cropped form. The homepage credits every author
and links each source (`src/features/home/reel.ts`).
