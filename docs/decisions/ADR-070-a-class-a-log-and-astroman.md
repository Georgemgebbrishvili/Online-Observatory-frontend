# ADR-070 — A Class, a Log, and Astroman

- **Date:** 2026-10-23
- **Status:** APPROVED (maintainer's standing approval for Phase 6 scope records, given
  2026-10-23; open to revision on review)
- **Decided by:** project maintainer
- **Relates to:** Phase 6 plan, slice 6.8; ADR-036 (ten observer seats); ADR-052 (quiet
  visual language); the maintainer's request of 2026-10-23 for a lean product

## Decision

### 1. A class watches through what exists

A teacher books a night like anyone and opens it to the class with observer seats
(ADR-036, ten at most). For the projector, the session room has a **Presenter view**
(`?present`): the state of the session and the feed, large, with everything else stepped
aside and a link back. Group booking and printed worksheets are deferred: the seats already
let a class in, and worksheets need content the maintainer writes.

### 2. The observer log is a tally, not a game

The Collection's first page shows **Your log**: each kind the catalogue offers and how many
of its targets the customer has a capture of ("2 of 4" planets). Counted from their
missions' captures. No badges, no streaks, no illustration. When simulated sessions are
counted, it says so.

### 3. Astroman, in one line

The target page and a capture's page say **Seen through a Celestron NexStar 8SE**, the
observatory's own telescope as the platform reports it, with a link to astroman.ge in a new
tab. No prints are sold here; that would need a checkout this slice does not add.

## Consequences

- Three small additions on pages that exist; no new route, no platform change.
